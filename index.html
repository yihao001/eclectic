Privacy within grasp

On-device model + local matching.

Eclectic is designed around local-only storage. Contents you create within the app - be it greetings, memories, lists, quotes, goals and reflections - stay on your device by default. Thus, no account creation is required.

Most of the app’s AI features are designed to work solely on-device without sending your data to any cloud service. Heavier tasks run externally without your data and the results are pulled into your device for local matching.

This approach ensures that your personal content never leaves your fingertips.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about how Eclectic works and what makes it different.

What is Eclectic?

Eclectic is a privacy-first companion for everyday life, customised to the Singaporean context. It brings things you use in daily life - to-do lists, goals, reflections, memories, groceries, F&B, quotes, weather - into one place and it uses the latest local / on-device AI models to help you manage them efficiently.

Why create Eclectic?

While the most common way most people interact with AI is through a chat application running in the cloud, it is not the only way. On-device / Edge AI, i.e. running smaller AI models directly on your device without relying on cloud infrastructure, is an emerging area that is only going to become more exciting in the coming years. While your phone is certainly not as powerful as a GPU server, several interesting advancements have made on-device AI practically usable for many real-world use cases. One area where this can be particularly impactful is personal productivity, where AI can assist you for daily routines, directly on your device without compromising your privacy. Eclectic is one way to achieve this.

There is a particular emphasis on creating local solutions (as in tailored to the Singaporean context) as no one else is going to do it for us. AI is undoubtedly transformational but it comes with biases that have to be patched and many last-mile developments have to be done to ensure that people are not left out. Again, Eclectic is one way to achieve this and hopefully this inspires more local initiatives in the same vein.

Do I need to pay?

No. Eclectic is free and will remain so as long as I am around.

How do you stay afloat?

With plenty of careful planning and engineering. The principle will always be to provide a free and privacy-first experience for all users of this app. Very reluctant to introduce ads ; if it has to happen, it will be siloed and as discreet as possible. Let's see.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Eclectic is designed to work without an account. Your personal lists, notes and goals are stored locally on your device by default.

Does Eclectic send my personal content to the cloud?

No. Your personal content stays on your device by default. OCR and AI features run on-device, so your quotes and notes are not sent to any remote AI service. There are requests made to pull information from APIs, but these requests do not include your personal data.

While personal content is never sent out, Google's ML Kit could transmit some non-personal data to Google in the following ways: (i) Performance metrics: ML Kit sends usage and performance metrics about how the APIs function within the app to Google. (ii) Device and app info: SDKs collect basic app and device details for diagnostics and analytics. (iii) Server communication: APIs occasionally contact Google servers to download bug fixes, updated models, or hardware information.

Play store requirements require apps with generative AI to provide a way to flag inappropriate content. If you submit this Google form, the input to the model, generated content, type of operation (e.g. category suggestion) and model type will be prefilled to allow targetted remediations. These are very unlikely to contain any personal data but you may remove any info you consider sensitive. Submitting the form is entirely optional.

What do I need to run the local AI models?

The most efficient way (and the default set in the app) is to use ML Kit, which works on most modern smartphones (Android 5.0 and above, iOS 15.5 and above). This should not require any setup nor additional storage space. Text generation features through ML Kit are available for a smaller subset of devices.

Alternatively, you may choose to download other models through the 'Manage local models' screen found in the Hamburger menu of the app. These models generally require about 4-7GB of storage space depending on the specific model you choose, so make sure you have enough free space on your device before downloading them. Most models would take about 3-10s for simple queries (e.g. categories) but it could takes about a minute in the worst-case scenario for more complex queries like filling up all fields.

How do weather updates differ from what NEA provides?

There are 2 key differences: (i) Raw information about weather e.g. WBGT Temp, Humidity, etc. are obtained from NEA's Realtime Weather Readings and other related endpoints. You will find that most, if not all, of the readings will match with what they show on their website / app. What Eclectic does is to allow you to view the data subset most relevant to you once you choose a location or enter a postal code and we present them in a user-friendly format. (ii) Rain predictions are generated based on our own models. It offers a 10-mins heads-up, which is more granular and localised compared to the broader 2-hour forecasts provided by NEA.

How accurate are the rain predictions?

tldr: CSI ~ 0.5, which is quite typical for the kind of model we can feasibly deploy without incurring heavy computational costs. This means "correctly predicted 50% of the rainfall events that either occurred or were expected to occur"; in other words, TP / (TP + FP + FN)

Short-term rainfall predictions, i.e. nowcasting, is very challenging in Singapore's tropical climate. Here's one explanation from Gemini. "Because Singapore is a small island surrounded by water, the land heats up much faster than the sea during the morning. This creates localized sea breezes that blow inland from both the southern coast and the northern coast (across the Johor Strait) simultaneously. By afternoon, these opposing sea breezes collide dead-center over the island. This generates a hyper-local convergence line that can suddenly spawn heavy downpours over places with zero warning." As of mid September 2026, CSI is about 0.5 which is modest, but we expect this to get better over the coming months as it becomes feasible to use better models.

Can I use the app without camera or notification permissions?

Yes. Camera access is only needed for optional quote OCR, and notifications are only used for reminders you enable. You can keep using the rest of the app without either permission.

How do I delete my data?

You can edit or delete content in the app, use its data-management controls under settings, or clear the app’s data and uninstall it through your device settings. Please export anything important first, because locally deleted content practically cannot be recovered.
